Performance KPIs to Track

  • CTR — link clicks ÷ impressions per channel
  • CPC — cost per click (if running paid ads)
  • Conversion rate — clicks → deal page views → affiliate outbound clicks
  • Revenue — affiliate commissions attributed per UTM source
  • Bounce rate by channel (quality of traffic)

UTM Naming Convention

  • utm_source: platform (instagram, whatsapp, tiktok, facebook, newsletter, telegram)
  • utm_medium: format (story, post, bio, status, broadcast, video, email)
  • utm_campaign: always blackfriday2026

Add &utm_content={creative-id} for A/B test variants.

Tip: Use these links in your Instagram bio (Linktree/beacons), Story swipe-ups, WhatsApp status updates, and TikTok bio. Track performance in your analytics (GA4/Plausible) by filtering on utm_campaign=blackfriday2026 and grouping by utm_source / utm_medium.
