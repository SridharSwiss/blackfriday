Timing

When prices move

  1. Now – 10.11.Build your list

    Set a target price per product. Several record lows, like AirPods Pro 3 and Switch 2, happened outside November.

  2. Mi 11.11.Singles Day

    Smaller discounts in Switzerland. Last year it brought the MacBook Air M4 to CHF 699 at DayDeal.

  3. Mo 23.11.Black Week opens

    Digitec Galaxus, Brack and Interdiscount run deals all week. Limited stock sells out early in the morning.

  4. Fr 27.11.Black Friday

    Peak sales day. Deals go live at midnight; DayDeal and Digitec drops are usually gone before noon.

  5. Mo 30.11.Cyber Monday

    Swiss shops mostly extend their Black Friday prices until here rather than launch new ones.

Buying rules

Don't get played

  • Compare against last Black WeekSome shops raise prices shortly before Black Friday to inflate the discount. Each tag shows what the product really cost in Black Week 2025.
  • Join loyalty programmes before the dayManor, H&M and Ochsner Sport reserve parts of their Black Friday offers for members.
  • Read the Amazon fine printNot every Amazon.de item ships to Switzerland, some arrive with a Schuko plug or German keyboard, and Prime-only prices need Prime.
  • First-order codes countSeveral record lows (fnac, Samsung Store) relied on a one-time newsletter or new-customer code.
  • Buy last year's modelThe deepest cuts land on the previous generation: iPhone 16 and 17, Watch Series 10, MacBook Air M4.
