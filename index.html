Timing

When prices move

  1. Now – 10.11.Build your list

    Set a target price per product. Record lows on AirPods Pro 3 and Switch 2 already happened outside November.

  2. Mi 11.11.Singles Day

    A minor event in Switzerland, but some shops test codes. Fewer retailers take part each year.

  3. Mo 23.11.Black Week opens

    Digitec Galaxus, Brack and Interdiscount run deals across the whole week. Limited stock sells out early in the morning.

  4. Fr 27.11.Black Friday

    Peak sales day. Deals go live at midnight; DayDeal and Digitec drops are usually gone before noon.

  5. Mo 30.11.Cyber Monday

    Swiss shops mostly extend their Black Friday prices until here rather than launch new ones.

Buying rules

Don't get played

  • Check the price history, not the strike-throughSome shops raise prices shortly before Black Friday to inflate the discount. Compare against the record low on each tag.
  • Join loyalty programmes before the dayManor and Ochsner Sport reserve parts of their Black Friday offers for members.
  • Read the Amazon fine printNot every Amazon.de item ships to Switzerland, some arrive with a Schuko plug, and Prime-only prices need an active Prime membership.
  • First-order codes countSeveral record lows (fnac, Samsung Store) relied on a one-time newsletter or new-customer code.
  • Buy last year's modelThe deepest cuts land on the previous generation once a successor ships, like iPhone 17 after the iPhone 18 Pro launch.
