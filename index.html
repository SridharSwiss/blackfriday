Buying rules

Don't get played

  • Compare against last Black WeekSome shops raise prices shortly before Black Friday to inflate discounts.
  • Join loyalty programmes earlyManor, H&M and Ochsner Sport keep some offers for members.
  • Check delivery to SwitzerlandForeign shops may not ship every item, or use Schuko plugs and German keyboards.
  • First-order codes countSeveral record lows relied on newsletter or new-customer codes.
  • Buy last year's modelThe deepest cuts land on the previous generation.
